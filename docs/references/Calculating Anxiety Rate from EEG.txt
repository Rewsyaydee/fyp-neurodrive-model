Digital  Filtering  Modalities

 The  initial  step  in  the  preprocessing  pipeline  utilizes  a  high-pass  filter  to  establish  baseline

.
15
 The  necessity  of  this  filter  stems  from  low-frequency,  non-neural  signal  drifts  primarily  caused  by  galvanic  skin  responses,  such  as  sweat  accumulation  beneath  the

15

Conversely,  a  low-pass  filter  is  implemented  to  eradicate  high-frequency  artifacts,

Frequency-Domain  Paradigms  and  Spectral  Power  
Density
 The  fundamental  translation  of  the  cleaned,  continuous  EEG  time  series  into  a  mathematical  
representation
 
of
 
anxiety
 
is
 
achieved
 
through
 
frequency-domain
 
extraction.
 
The
 
time-domain
 
voltage
 
fluctuations
 
are
 
mathematically
 
transformed
 
into
 
a
 
frequency-domain
 
spectral
 
matrix
 
using
 
the
 
Fast
 
Fourier
 
Transform
 
(FFT)
 
or
 
Short-Time
 
Fourier
 
Transform
 
(STFT).
21
 This  algorithm  
yields
 
the
 
Power
 
Spectral
 
Density
 
(PSD),
 
quantifying
 
the
 
absolute
 
and
 
relative
 
distribution
 
of
 
signal
 
power
 
across
 
specific,
 
naturally
 
occurring
 
neural
 
oscillatory
 
bands.
6  
Neurophysiological  research  has  established  profound  correlations  between  anxiety  states  and  
alterations
 
in
 
these
 
specific
 
oscillatory
 
bands:
 
●  Delta  Waves  (  to  ):  Slow-wave  (SW)  oscillations  primarily  generated  by  subcortical  structures  involved  in  motivation,  reward  processing,  and  primary  emotional  
drive.
 
Increased
 
delta
 
activity,
 
particularly
 
when
 
observed
 
in
 
pathological
 
formations,
 
is
 
shaped
 
by
 
heightened
 
anxiety
 
states
 
and
 
the
 
psychomotor
 
retardation
 
associated
 
with
 
comorbid
 
depressive
 
disorders.
2  
●  Theta  Waves  (  to  ):  Mid-frontal  theta  activity  provides  a  direct  neural  index  of  the  brain's  processing  of  social  repudiation,  conflict  detection,  and  
cognitive-affective
 
regulation.
 
Anxiety
 
states
 
invariably
 
trigger
 
elevated
 
theta
 
oscillations
 
in
 
the
 
prefrontal
 
cortex,
 
indicating
 
an
 
overburdened
 
regulatory
 
mechanism
 
attempting
 
to
 
process
 
emotional
 
stimuli.
2  
●  Alpha  Waves  (  to  ):  The  alpha  rhythm  serves  as  an  inverse  proxy  for  regional  cortical  activation;  high  alpha  power  denotes  a  state  of  cortical  idling  or  
relaxation,
 
whereas
 
decreased
 
alpha
 
power
 
signifies
 
active
 
cognitive
 
processing
 
or
 
stress.
2
 Diminished  slow-wave  alpha  is  broadly  linked  to  elevated  baseline  stress  and  high  
state
 
anxiety.
 
Conversely,
 
therapeutic
 
interventions
 
that
 
reduce
 
self-reported
 
anxiety
 
typically
 
yield
 
a
 
global
 
enhancement
 
in
 
cortical
 
alpha
 
amplitude.
24  
●  Beta  Waves  (  to  ):  Fast-wave  (FW)  oscillations  denote  active  intercortical  communication  and  are  primarily  engaged  during  executive  functioning,  
attentional
 
control,
 
and
 
problem-solving.
2
 Pathological  anxiety  correlates  heavily  with  
global
 
increases
 
in
 
beta
 
overactivity,
 
particularly
 
midline
 
overactivity
 
within
 
the
 
anterior
 
cingulate
 
cortex,
 
a
 
region
 
intimately
 
linked
 
to
 
cognitive
 
rigidity
 
and
 
excessive
 
worry.
24
